# Project Goals ʕʘ̅͜ʘ̅ʔ

I am planning to make a magic challenge inspired by Black Clover. I love the idea of anime and also hand movements as a way to perform a magic spell and have some magical book to use in performing the spell.

The Centroid Finder server and video pipeline will be the basis for my solution. Tracking bare-hand movement would make a unique feature, and the backend of the project would analyze it and determine a result.

## Easy

I would begin by taking a small recorded video and track one palm. The hand will be shown to move on the display with the help of hand-tracking, while the backend of the system would tell if the hand got close enough to a certain point on the screen. The system will provide a message to tell whether the hand succeeded or failed.

## Goal >ᴗ<

Track two hands in a recorded video; each hand traces half of a
magic circle while the movement paths are colored purple or different color :) (btw I love purple).
Once the video is played and processed, the backend will evaluate and
return the completeness of the magic circle. Depending on whether the
player passes, the player will either unlock the spellbook or get to view the traced paths again.

## Stretch

Track live webcam footage instead of video footage. Also include more shapes and a possibility to
keep previous results; also implement spellbook navigation using hand
gestures.

## Impossible

Real-time online full-body multiplayer magic battle, 3D magic spells, and many gestures in a real time environment.
